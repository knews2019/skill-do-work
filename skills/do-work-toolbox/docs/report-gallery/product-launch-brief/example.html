The reader’s collection

After saving a postcard, a reader can find it by its illustration and reopen the excerpt. Here’s how the first saved card would appear.

How the newsroom makes the card

  1. Choose the excerpt

    Assigning editor

    Check the ferry excerpt against the reporting and approve a version that makes sense on its own. Flag the card if the story is corrected.

  2. Illustrate and assemble

    Visual designer

    Pair the approved excerpt with the ferry illustration and write alt text for the front.

  3. Check and schedule

    Audience producer

    On a phone, flip The First Ferry, save it, and reopen it from the collection before scheduling the card.

Decisions before launch

  • When a story is corrected, who updates the excerpt on cards readers already saved?
  • Can the visual desk supply an original or licensed image and alt text each weekday?
  • Who runs the phone check and schedules the card when the audience producer is away?
