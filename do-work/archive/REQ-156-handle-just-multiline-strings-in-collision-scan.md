## Exploration

The existing per-line header grammar, reserved-name derivation, managed-span exclusion, sorted intersection, diagnostic, and atomic replacement ordering remain correct.

Just 1.46 semantics require raw `'''` strings to close on the next literal delimiter regardless of backslashes, cooked `"""` strings to close only on an unescaped delimiter using contiguous-backslash parity, and continued suffix scanning after same-line closes/reopens. New openers must be ignored in comments, indented recipe bodies, and ordinary quoted tokens. ## Root Cause

`just_definition_names()` previously classified each physical line independently, so column-zero payload inside a valid multiline Just variable string was indistinguishable from a real recipe or alias definition.

## Decisions

- **D-01 — Keep the established definition grammar.** Add only cross-line triple-string state around `just_definition_name()`; leave reserved-name derivation, managed-span exclusion, sorted diagnostics, and atomic replacement ordering unchanged.
- **D-02 — Match Just's two delimiter rules.** Close raw triple-single strings on the next literal delimiter and close cooked triple-double strings only when the delimiter has even contiguous-backslash parity; continue scanning suffixes for same-line close/reopen behavior.
- **D-03 — Prove both directions.** Use Just-parseable positive fixtures for string payload and an exact, byte-preserving negative control for real definitions immediately around a multiline value.

## Lessons Learned

**What worked:**
- Keeping the existing header classifier intact made the requested triple-string repair small and preserved exact collision behavior around the new lexical state.
- Just-parseable positive fixtures plus byte-preserving negative controls proved both acceptance and safety through the production helper.

**What didn't:**
- The initial durable summary generalized from the two requested triple-quoted forms to all multiline Just values. Independent review reproduced the same false rejection for three adjacent literal forms; the final summary wording is narrowed and REQ-159 records the remaining class.

**Worth knowing:**
- Once a handwritten safety scanner owns a lexer boundary, adjacent valid syntax needs explicit adversarial coverage even when the requested defect names only one token family.
