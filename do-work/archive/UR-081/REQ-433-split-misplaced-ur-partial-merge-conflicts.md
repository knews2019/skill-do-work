## Lessons Learned

**What worked:** Retaining one fail-closed directory validation and splitting only the returned operations narrowed conflict coupling without weakening unsafe-tree handling.
**What didn't:** A directory-shaped move group accidentally made an ordinary destination conflict contagious to every sibling in that directory.
**Worth knowing:** Safety validation and conflict-domain granularity are separate decisions; validate the full source tree once, then give independently movable items independent groups.
