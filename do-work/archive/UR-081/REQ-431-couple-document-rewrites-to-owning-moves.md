## Exploration

The defect is confined to cleanup's link-enrichment and apply seams. Link enrichment currently builds one repository-wide move map, precomputes a whole-document replacement, and attaches it to the first group with a move. That loses conditional ownership and makes separately precomputed replacements overwrite rather than compose.

The frozen approach adds a typed per-group documentation-rewrite operation. Each eligible group carries only its own move mapping; apply reads current document bytes and rewrites at execution time, so independently eligible groups compose while a refused owner cannot publish its destination.

## Lessons Learned

**What worked:** Modeling each rewrite as an operation owned by its move group preserved conditional eligibility while current-byte application let successful groups compose naturally.
**What didn't:** One globally precomputed document replacement erased ownership boundaries and turned the first move group into an accidental proxy for every destination.
**Worth knowing:** When multiple conditional operations update one document, retain ownership in the plan and compose at execution time from current bytes inside the shared transaction.

## Orientation

Cleanup documentation rewrites now follow the exact move group that makes each destination valid. Successful groups compose safely in shared documents; refused or failed groups cannot publish links to absent destinations.
