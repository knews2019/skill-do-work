## Exploration

The defect is confined to cleanup's link-enrichment and apply seams. Link enrichment currently builds one repository-wide move map, precomputes a whole-document replacement, and attaches it to the first group with a move. That loses conditional ownership and makes separately precomputed replacements overwrite rather than compose.

The frozen approach adds a typed per-group documentation-rewrite operation. Each eligible group carries only its own move mapping; apply reads current document bytes and rewrites at execution time, so independently eligible groups compose while a refused owner cannot publish its destination.
