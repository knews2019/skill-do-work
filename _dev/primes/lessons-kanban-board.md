- [family: wrapping-flex-item-base-width] external review of the 20 commits ending at `37b61ca1` (2026-09-05, no REQ — fixed directly, released as 0.303.10): **a wrapping flex item's flex base is its UNWRAPPED width**, which makes the row permanently in deficit and lets flexbox shrink its sibling that had room. `.board-controls` wraps, so its `auto` basis is every control group on one line; the first attempt at the top-bar overflow fix put `min-width: 0` on `.board-identity` and the identity was ellipsised at 1000px where 96px were free. `flex: 1 1 0` on the wrapping side is the fix — it takes what the other item does not want and wraps inside it — and it must be reset to `0 1 auto` inside the stacking media query, where a zero basis is a zero HEIGHT.
