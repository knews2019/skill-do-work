## Borrow

- Rank the edits by the strength of their evidence. Give each the same benefit, cost and risk fields, including the option to defer.
- Put a real request alongside the rule history. Show what the verifier corrected and what the reviewer discovered after the tests passed.
- Fold exact edit text, repository locations and optional commands behind the decision.
