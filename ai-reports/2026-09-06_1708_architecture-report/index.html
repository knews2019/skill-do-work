VERIFIED · A10

Recovery resolves finalization before inspecting claims.

recover invokes finalization discovery first, then rediscovers working requests. Ordinary recovery preserves claims lacking takeover authority and returns a specific remedy. run-with-recovery is a deliberate authority-bearing action. Heavy verification is a held phase of claimed work.

After review, a selected heavy-test plan and landed commit keep the REQ claimed and make its source available to dependents. At queue exhaustion, the action drains selected lanes. A green result finalizes; red results withdraw commit readiness and enter remediation. A skipped lane is not a pass.
