After the report

The canvas was tried the same day, and removed

Version 0.316.0 of do-work added a Tinka canvas offer to every report, with a hand recipe. Version 0.317.0 removed both. Its changelog entry gives the reason: a real canvas showed each report page as a thin strip that cannot be read.

That is K1 in practice. On the map, K1 is the only kill attempt that limits both O2 and O3 and that only O4 removes.
