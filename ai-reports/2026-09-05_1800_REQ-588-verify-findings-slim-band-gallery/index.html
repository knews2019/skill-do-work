Verify findings as a slim band

2026-09-05 · three options · every state of each captured at board width, light and dark · nothing here has shipped

Each option is a live page in the board's own stylesheet with the real top bar, the real four columns, and today's findings. The frames are interactive; the captures beneath them show every state so nothing is left to imagine.

Verdict

The strip was huge because it printed every remedy all the time inside a card. A remedy is what you read after deciding to act, so it belongs behind one click. What stays visible is short: a subject, a lowercase category, one line of detail, and a dot for weight (amber: read this, green: cleanup can fix, grey: a probe that never ran).

M4 is the recommendation. Closed it is a 34 px line that still names every subject; open it is one 28 px row per finding; a chevron opens a remedy. M5 is M4 permanently open. M6 is the smallest, one line of pills, but hides the detail until you click.

M4 · one bar recommended

Closed: one line. Open: one row per finding.

The header line carries the label, the counts, every subject with its weight dot, and a Show button. Opening it swaps the subject list for rows: dot, subject in mono, category, the detail clipped at the line's end, a chevron. The chevron opens that row's remedy in a quiet inset block. Show/Hide is remembered per browser, so a reader who wants the strip closed sees one line on every visit.
