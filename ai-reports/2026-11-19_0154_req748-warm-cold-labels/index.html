How to interpret the labels

Each selection contributes separate groups of three cold and three warm runs to its comparison. Run ordinals identify observations within each group. The design provides no replicate pairing or estimate of a causal cache effect. The cold branch cleared the Go test cache, and Go selections already used -count=1.
