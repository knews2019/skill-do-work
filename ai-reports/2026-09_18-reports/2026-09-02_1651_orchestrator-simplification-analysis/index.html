Simplifying the do-work Orchestrator

2026-09-02 · target: the run action as it stands at commit 721c2fb4 · evidence mode: non-visual

Verdict. The orchestrator can drop roughly half of its run-action prose without changing behavior. About 55% of work.md's step text describes mechanics the Go CLI already owns or is about to own. The thing holding that prose in place is not its value but the contract suite that pins its sentences. The way through is one mechanical step per REQ: move it into a CLI command, delete its prose, delete its sentence-predicates, add a behavior test. Not a rewrite.

Prose that describes mechanics

≈55% of step lines (about 390 of 709) are mechanical or the mechanical half of a mixed step.

Three steps carry it: Find Next (95), Archive (66), Loop or Exit (155). Together they are 45% of all step prose and they are exactly what next, complete, finalize and recover-finalization do.

Prose that must stay

Route judgment, planning, the Finding-Closure Ratchet, review depth, Fold-First minting, lessons, changelog prose, and the ten Common Rationalizations tables. That is the part an agent cannot get from a program, and it fits in roughly 150 lines once the mechanics leave.

3 · Why the prose has not shrunk on its own

Mechanics have moved into Go before. Selection, claim, archive and release are all CLI transactions today. The prose stayed each time because the test for the prose stayed. The contract suite pins sentences with mutation-tested predicates, so deleting a paragraph fails a lane, and a builder under review keeps the paragraph.

The rule that unlocks deletion

A step leaves prose only as a unit: the CLI command that owns it, the deleted paragraph, the deleted sentence-predicates, and a new behavior test on the command. Any one of the four without the others recreates today's state. The REQ that moves a step names all four in its write set, so the review can refuse a half-move.

The floor agent gets better, not worse

CLAUDE.md requires the simplest agent that can read files and run shell to follow the action. A numbered 850-line procedure is hard for that agent. A CLI that prints the exact next command and the evidence still missing is easier, because the agent no longer has to hold the state machine in its head.

4 · The shape after

One new command, do-work-cli advance REQ-NNN, composes the commands that already exist into a lifecycle state machine. It reports the current phase for the REQ's route, the evidence still missing, and the next command, and it refuses a transition with a typed finding. work.md becomes a loop of one sentence plus principles: ask where you are, do the judgment the phase needs, record the evidence, ask again.

What work.md keeps

The loop sentence. Route criteria. When to ask versus decide. The Finding-Closure Ratchet. Review depth by impact. Fold-First minting. The one-broken-pipe principle. Communication style. Each keyed on its condition, with examples marked illustrative.

What work-reference.md keeps

The Execution Model, the schema read contract, and templates reduced to a minimal example that points at the Go validator. Sections that restate a CLI contract go with the step they served.

5 · Sequence, one REQ per move

  1. Read-only advance. Reports phase, missing evidence, next command. Changes no behavior, so it lands without touching prose. Everything else depends on it.
  2. Loop, checkpoint and session start. After REQ-498, REQ-499 and REQ-501 land, Step 10 and Crash Recovery collapse to "run recovery; the result says what to do". This is the largest single deletion, 155 lines plus the reference section.
  3. Selection and claim. Steps 1, 2.0 and 2 become advance phases; the selection principle stays as one paragraph.
  4. Evidence gates. Estimate, pre-flight, qualify and the test gate run from advance; the anti-rationalization table and the Ratchet stay as principles.
  5. Archive and commit tails. Steps 8 and 9 hand to finalize; Fold-First minting stays prose.
  6. Templates. capture-reference.md keeps one minimal example per record and points at the schema layer for every rule it now enforces.
  7. Crew members. The ten Common Rationalizations tables merge into one crew member loaded at implementation.
  8. Reference sweep. Delete every work-reference.md section whose contract is now a CLI behavior test.

Not this

A one-shot rewrite of work.md. The suite goes red in hundreds of places at once and a real regression becomes indistinguishable from a deleted sentence. The unit is one step, one REQ, with the four-part write set.

Risks

R1 Hard-won traps live in sentence-predicates; each move must carry its trap into a Go test or a lessons satellite. R2 advance must not become a second prose surface; its output is typed findings, not paragraphs. R3 Two orchestrators against one queue during the migration; run it in one session.

7 · Open questions

Q1 Does advance live in core or as a thin composition layer in the toolbox? Core, because the run action is core, but it is a real choice. Q2 Which lanes of the contract suite are traps worth keeping as Go tests versus prose that only ever guarded prose? That is decided per move, not up front. Q3 Should REQ-498's stale "stop after one slice" constraint get a queued addendum before its builder claims it? Yes, and it is not done yet.
