Release batching · Separate decision

Compare policy choices without assuming the cause.

Fact: current policy makes each shipped-change commit a release. Consumers fetch a main archive and skip equal versions. A batching design must keep version identity coherent, for example by isolating released content.

Corrected queue claim: the seven queued defects comprise three release-guard fixes, three commit-integrity/recovery fixes, and one output-diagnostics fix. Batching does not automatically eliminate the latter four—or establish which guards are redundant.

Policy choice: retain releases per shipped-change commit, or evaluate batching with an explicit distribution design. R3 changes history packaging only.
